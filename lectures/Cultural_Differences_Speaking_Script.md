# Cultural Differences — Speaking Script

Hello everyone!

Today, I'm going to talk about cultural differences.

Culture is an important part of our lives. It includes our language, food, traditions, values, and the way we communicate with other people. Culture can influence even small things we do every day.

First, let's talk about greetings. In Japan, bowing is a common way to greet someone and show respect. In many other countries, people shake hands. Some people greet friends with a hug or a kiss on the cheek. These greetings may look different, but they can all express kindness and respect.

Food is another interesting example. Every country and region has its own traditional dishes and eating habits. In parts of South Asia, for example, many people traditionally eat some meals with their hands. In other places, people usually use forks, spoons, or chopsticks. Food is more than something we eat: it can tell us about a country's history and identity.

We can also see cultural differences in celebrations. Nowruz is the Persian New Year, celebrated at the beginning of spring. Families may prepare a Haft-Seen table and visit one another. Christmas is celebrated by many people on December 25. Common traditions include decorating a tree, sharing meals, and giving presents. The celebrations are different, but both can bring families and communities together.

There are also differences in daily communication. Ideas about being on time, personal space, eye contact, and body language can vary from culture to culture. It is important not to judge someone too quickly. We should learn what a gesture or habit means before making assumptions.

In conclusion, cultural differences make our world interesting. Different does not mean wrong. When we respect other cultures and stay curious, we can understand people better and build stronger friendships.

Thank you for listening!
